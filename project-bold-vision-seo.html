Commercial vehicle wrap

Bold Vision SEO

A custom wrap that turns a Chevrolet Duramax pickup into a clear, professional introduction to Bold Vision SEO.

Project
Bold Vision SEO
Focus
Custom commercial wrap

The idea

Make the business easy to understand at a glance. Bold Vision SEO’s name, search marketing message, and contact information needed to read clearly on a truck seen both parked and in motion.

The approach

We arranged the logo, service message, phone number, and QR code around the pickup’s doors, bed, rear window, and tailgate. The orange and blue graphics connect those surfaces while keeping the essential information easy to find.
