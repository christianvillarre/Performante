Why It Pays Off

The Business Case For Wrapping Your Fleet

Every mile your vehicles drive is free advertising — if the vehicle is dressed for it.

01

Lowest Cost-Per-Impression

A wrap generates tens of thousands of daily impressions for a one-time cost, working out to fractions of a cent per view over its lifespan.

02

Consistent Brand Presence

Matching graphics across every vehicle reinforce recognition each time a customer sees a truck on site or on the highway.

03

Often Tax-Deductible

Vehicle wraps are typically treated as a deductible advertising expense — confirm the specifics with your accountant.

04

Built For The Road

Commercial-grade film and laminate handle daily driving, weather and regular washing without fading or lifting.

05

Scales With Your Fleet

Add a vehicle next year and we match it to your existing design on file — no redesigning from scratch.

06

Zero Paint Risk

A wrap protects the factory finish underneath, so a leased or resale vehicle comes out from under it undamaged.

How It Works

From Survey To Install, In Five Stops

  1. 01

    Consultation & Vehicle Survey

    We measure and photograph every vehicle in your fleet, or work from spec sheets for vehicles not yet delivered.

  2. 02

    Design Proof

    Your brand guide becomes a wrap-ready layout. You review and approve proofs before anything goes to print.

  3. 03

    Print & Laminate

    Graphics are printed in-house and laminated for UV and abrasion resistance built for daily driving.

  4. 04

    Staggered Installation

    Vehicles are scheduled a few at a time so the rest of your fleet stays on the road while we work.

  5. 05

    Maintenance Plan

    An optional recurring check-up catches lifting edges and panel wear before they become a re-wrap.
