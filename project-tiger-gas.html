Custom vehicle wrap

Tiger Gas

A custom cab wrap that gives this Ram 5500 Heavy Duty a bold Tiger Gas identity from every approach.

Project
Tiger Gas
Focus
Custom cab graphics & wrap

The idea

Make Tiger Gas instantly recognizable on a working truck. A large tiger illustration anchors the doors, while the company name and contact details remain clear across the cab.

The approach

The design brings tiger stripes, stars, and textured graphics across the hood and cab. Each element was sized for the Ram 5500’s panels, creating a cohesive look while leaving the chassis ready for its working configuration.
