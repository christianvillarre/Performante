Custom vehicle wrap

Tiger Gas

A high-impact, full-body wrap that turns the truck into a moving expression of the Tiger Gas brand.

Project
Tiger Gas
Focus
Custom graphics & vehicle wrap

The idea

The design uses strong color, layered pattern, and the truck's broad side profile to make the identity legible at a glance. The vehicle becomes the canvas, rather than a place to simply apply a logo.

The approach

Graphic scale and placement follow the shape of the body, carrying the visual language across doors, panels, and transitions for a complete wrap experience.
