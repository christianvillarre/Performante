The Business Case For Wrapping Your Fleet

Every mile your vehicles drive is free advertising — if the vehicle is dressed for it.

01

Lowest Cost-Per-Impression

A wrap generates tens of thousands of daily impressions for a one-time cost — fractions of a cent per view over its lifespan.

02

Consistent Brand Presence

Matching graphics across every vehicle reinforce recognition each time a customer sees a truck on site or on the highway.

03

Built For The Road

Commercial-grade film and laminate handle daily driving, weather and washing without fading or lifting.

04

Scales With Your Fleet

Add a vehicle next year and we match it to your existing design on file — no redesigning from scratch.

05

Minimal Downtime

Vehicles are scheduled a few at a time in staggered windows, so the rest of your fleet stays on the road while we work.
