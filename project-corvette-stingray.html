Custom vehicle wrap

Corvette Stingray

A one-of-a-kind camouflage wrap created specifically for a 2021 Corvette Stingray.

Project
2021 Corvette Stingray
Focus
Custom camo design & wrap

The idea

Create a camouflage design with its own identity while respecting the Stingray’s dramatic shape. The layered green, tan, and black pattern gives the car a distinctive look from every angle.

The approach

We developed and sized the artwork for this vehicle, then planned its placement across the hood, doors, rear quarters, and bumper. Each section was fitted to the Corvette’s contours so the pattern carries cleanly from panel to panel.
